churn: set true when the interview looks truncated — the patient disengaged before their story resolved. Judge this mainly from how the transcript ENDS:
- Set true when it ends mid-sentence or mid-word with no resolution (e.g. cut off at "Chronic..."), i.e. a genuine cut-off.
- Treat a trailing "..." as a cut-off ONLY if "..." does NOT appear elsewhere in the transcript as a stylistic/pausing device. If "..." recurs as a habit, 
or the final sentence reads as a complete (even if downbeat or emotional) ending, set false — a detailed, engaged narrative that closes on a complete sentence 
is NOT churn even if it trails off in tone.
- When the ending is ambiguous and there is no clear structural signal of a cut-off, prefer false (the transcript alone under-determines app-disengagement).
Set false when the interview reads as a reasonably complete journey. Keep this truncation signal distinct from a topic simply being absent from an otherwise 
complete story (that is "not mentioned", not churn).

Use NO_BIOLOGIC for every treatment when the patient never had a biologic prescribed, offered, or mentioned (there is no biologic to anchor the split). Use UNKNOWN only when a biologic exists but you cannot tell whether this treatment came before or after it.
